Help Center

🛒 How to Order

  • Browse products on the Shop page or use the search bar to find specific items.
  • Click "Add to Cart" on any product you want to purchase.
  • Go to your cart and review your items, then click "Proceed to Checkout".
  • Fill in your delivery details (name, email, phone, and address).
  • Select your preferred payment method (eSewa or Bank Payment).
  • Click "Proceed to Payment" to complete your purchase.

💳 Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods in Nepali Rupees (Rs):

  • eSewa: Pay instantly using your eSewa wallet. Payment is confirmed immediately and your order status will show as "Completed".
  • Bank Payment: Pay directly from your bank account. Enter your bank details on the payment page and confirm the payment.

🚚 Delivery Information

  • Free shipping on orders over Rs 3,000.
  • Standard shipping fee of Rs 150 for orders below Rs 3,000.
  • Delivery within Kathmandu Valley: 1-2 business days.
  • Delivery outside Kathmandu Valley: 3-5 business days.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your order is shipped.

↩️ Return & Refund Policy

  • You can return items within 7 days of delivery if they are damaged or defective.
  • Items must be in original packaging with all accessories included.
  • Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.
  • Contact us before returning any item for a return authorization.

👤 Account & Login

  • Create an account by clicking "Sign Up" and entering your email and password.
  • Use the same credentials to login on future visits.
  • Logged-in users can view their order history on the "My Orders" page.
  • You can also checkout as a guest without creating an account.
